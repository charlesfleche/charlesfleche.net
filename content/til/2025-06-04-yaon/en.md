Title: Presentation of Ynput's Ayon at the USDrinks
Description: An Open Source VFX pipeline

Today during the USD meeting at [Moment Factory](http://www.momentfactory.com/), [Robin De Lillo](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm6570148/) shown us [Ayon](https://ynput.io/ayon/), an Open Source pipeline for VFX / animation / creative studios. It looks quite neat, and I like that they develop it in the open, fully on GitHub, with features funded by paying customers. I'd love to have a proper demo of their product some times in the future.
